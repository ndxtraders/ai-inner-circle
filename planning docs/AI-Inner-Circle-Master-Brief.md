## 1. What This Is

**AI Inner Circle** is a membership that helps founder-led B2B service businesses *implement* AI systems for attracting, engaging, and converting buyers.

**The core positioning — memorize this, it drives every decision:**

> This is **not** "learn AI." It's **implement skills/agents**. **Problem first, AI solution second.** We are not chasing shiny objects — every drop fixes a specific bleeding-neck problem the member already feels.

### Membership mechanics
- **$49/mo** (Community) — full curriculum, workshops, weekly Q&A, daily community access
- **$800/mo** (Community + 1:1) — adds weekly personal coaching
- **Cadence:** one new skill/agent drops **every Tuesday** · **1–2 workshops/month** · **Friday Q&A with Rev** · daily community access
- **Arc:** a 6-month curriculum where skills **compound into a full system**, not a pile of disconnected tools

### Who it's for
Busy, founder-led B2B service businesses. The founder is the face/voice of the brand **and the bottleneck** — they make every important decision, most processes live in their head, nothing is systematized. We are deliberately **not micro-niching** yet; the plan is built for the general "busy bottlenecked founder," not one vertical.

**Known gap (now fixed in this plan):** the assessment diagnoses breaking points the old curriculum never treated — especially **Team Work** and **Client Experience**. The revised curriculum below closes that gap so the assessment's promises match what the membership delivers.

## 3. The Big Idea: Authority Operating System

We reframed from "skill library" to an **Authority Operating System (AI-OS)**. Members don't "take a course" — they **hire an agent** to fix one bleeding-neck problem at a time. The skills accumulate into a business that runs a full loop (attract → convert → deliver → retain) without living in the founder's head.

### Settled strategic decisions
1. **Implementation over information.** Problem-first, AI-second. This is the top marketing message.
2. **Orchestrator + memory come FIRST**, in onboarding — because "I only use skills when I remember them" is the exact problem the membership solves. The coordination layer can't be the finale.
3. **Lead with a fast, visible pipeline win (Month 1).** The membership sells on urgency ("breaking point"); time-to-first-visible-win is the #1 retention lever, so it cannot sit at Month 4.
4. **Cheap + safe outbound: Apify + LinkedIn, not Apollo/HubSpot/cold email.** Enterprise CRM stacks are expensive and email outreach burns domains when best practices aren't followed. Apify run in **small batches is pay-as-you-go and very affordable** (cheaper than a $49 subscription). LinkedIn DMs are a closed loop — a bad message can't torch your primary email domain. Members may need a paid LinkedIn subscription.
5. **Every month maps to an assessment pillar**, so the diagnostic sells the exact fix the member then receives.
6. **Not limited to the current skill library.** Any skill can be built. ~70% of what's needed already exists on Rev's machine (see §6).

## 7. Open Questions / Next Decisions

- **Content vs. outreach order (M3 vs. M4-style motion):** current plan runs authority content (M3) before deep conversion (M4). There's a live argument to interleave signal + outreach + content rather than silo them. Revisit after first cohort data.
- **Skill cognitive load:** 24 skills in 24 weeks is a firehose for an overwhelmed founder (realistic absorption ≈ 1–2/month). Consider framing: 1 hero skill/month (workshop centerpiece) + 3 micro-drops with explicit permission to skip.
- **Advanced-ops scope creep:** War Gaming, Decision Memos, custom-agent "hiring manager" skew toward power users. Audit against the honest ICP before committing build time.
- **Built-in "capture your win" ritual:** not yet designed. Needed for retention *and* to generate the testimonials/case studies that sell the next cohort.
- **Workshop titles + sales-page promises:** not yet drafted (this is task "B").

## 8. Glossary of Referenced Concepts
- **Bleeding neck** — the single most painful, urgent problem; where we start because it's easiest to show fast ROI.
- **AI slop** — generic, robotic AI output; killed via the voice/humanizer skills so content sounds like the founder.
- **Signal-based outreach** — reaching people based on real behavior (e.g., commenting on a relevant post) instead of cold spray.
- **Advance vs. continuation (Rackham/SPIN)** — the goal of lead gen is a *hand-raise / booked call* (an advance), not just "a lead."
